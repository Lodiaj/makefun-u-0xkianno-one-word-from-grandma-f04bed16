One word, and nothing else: the single word my grandma would text about AI.

**Alexa**

She calls every smart speaker, chatbot and robot by the one name she knows.
Ask her about AI and she will just say "Alexa" — warm, a little baffled, and
convinced that is what the whole thing is called.
